555 Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out 555 timer periods, pulses and resistor values from the numbers you type, using the standard chart constants 0.693 and 1.1. The resistances and capacitances are yours to measure or read off your own parts. It treats every component and chip as ideal, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the tolerances, the leakage, the supply or the chip variant, and it gives no verdict that a timer circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.
